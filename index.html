About

A new studio exploring useful, beautiful products in multiple mediums.

Kraken Ink is based in British Columbia and was created to develop products that blend practical utility with thoughtful design. The studio’s work spans digital products, creative tools, and physical goods made through modern fabrication methods.

The first digital product, BlueClip, is the result of months of focused development and marks the beginning of Kraken Ink’s product roadmap.
